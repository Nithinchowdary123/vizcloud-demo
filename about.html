Our approach

Recruitment built on empathy & trust

Understand first

Effective recruitment starts with empathy between our clients and us. We first understand the context and culture in which clients and candidates work — we won't accept an assignment until we fully understand the role's purpose, position, influence, accountabilities, and what constitutes success.

Tailored methodology

Every assignment is unique — dictated by time, business growth, strategy, and planned succession. We choose the most appropriate methodology, one that is both detailed and expedient, yet takes significant account of each client's operating imperatives, culture, and future direction.

Senior-led delivery

Each assignment is personally managed by a Senior Consultant whose industry knowledge has been gained over many years — as both a senior manager and an advising consultant. A shadow consultant and a researcher reinforce the process, ensuring transparency, focus, and a satisfactory result.

A trusted extension

Our modus operandi is to act as an extension of the client — a trusted recruitment partner. We pride ourselves on honesty, objectivity, and dedication to the brief, communicating frequently to ensure we stay aligned. We work with you, not only for you.
